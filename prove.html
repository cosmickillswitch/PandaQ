Logic

Premises go before ⊢, separated by commas. Leave them out to prove a theorem. ASCII works too: ~ & | -> <-> |-

Examples

Minimal

Only the introduction and elimination rules. ⊥ is just a formula with no special power, so “from a contradiction, anything” fails.

Intuitionistic

Adds ex falso (⊥E): from ⊥ infer anything. A proof of P ∨ Q must prove one side, so excluded middle and double-negation elimination are out.

Classical

Adds indirect proof (IP): if assuming ¬P leads to ⊥, conclude P. This unlocks excluded middle, Peirce’s law and the full De Morgan laws.
